# LogosForge Pro 0.1.5 — Alpha

> [!WARNING]
> This is alpha software. Keep independent backups of important work and export
> a `.lfbundle` regularly. The desktop packages are currently unsigned.

## What is new

- Adds first-class **Progressions** to the Story Bible for tracking story,
  character, relationship, theme, world, and custom narrative arcs as ordered
  beats with status, scene, chapter, and evidence links.
- Connects Progressions to the Narrative Knowledge Graph and Dashboard Decision
  Radar, including traceable evidence and direct graph deep links.
- Makes Progressions editing transactional: proposals carry revisions, stale
  writes are rejected, retries are idempotent, and accepted commands produce
  durable receipts.
- Stores pending Progressions commands and receipts atomically in Electron's
  stable user-data directory, so crash recovery survives renderer-origin
  changes, packaged-app restarts, and retry of the same proposal.
- Extends the authenticated local MCP gateway with Progressions discovery,
  context, proposal, apply, and recovery operations for external AI
  orchestrators.
- Preserves the detachable multi-monitor workspace, Skins, Timeline and Canvas
  transactions, Semantic Continuity, Guided Workflows, Controlled Apply, and
  the remaining 0.1.4 feature set.

## Platforms

- Windows x64: NSIS installer and portable executable.
- Linux x64: AppImage.
- macOS Intel x64: DMG for macOS 12 Monterey or newer.

Apple Silicon and universal macOS packages are not included. Windows SmartScreen
and macOS Gatekeeper may warn because these builds are not yet code-signed or
notarized. On macOS, right-click the app and choose **Open**, or clear quarantine
with `xattr -cr "/Applications/LogosForge Pro.app"`.

## Suggested manual acceptance

1. Create several progression tracks and beats, edit their order and statuses,
   and confirm the Story Bible retains them after switching panels and projects.
2. Open Decision Radar and the Knowledge Graph, confirm progression-backed cards
   expose their evidence, and follow their deep links to the correct graph data.
3. Detach Progressions and related panels to native windows, move them between
   monitors, restart Pro, and confirm both layout and progression data return.
4. Interrupt or close Pro while a progression proposal is pending, relaunch it,
   and confirm the durable receipt/recovery flow neither loses nor duplicates the
   accepted command.
5. Connect an MCP client, inspect progression context, submit a proposal, review
   it, and apply it only after the expected revision and mutation controls pass.

## Known limitations

- LogosForge Pro remains a local-first, single-user alpha without cloud sync or
  real-time collaboration. Web distribution is not part of this release.
- Hosted AI features require a configured provider such as a local LM Studio
  endpoint. Deterministic writing, planning, analysis, and export features stay
  available offline.
- Voice-model files are not bundled and must be supplied separately.
- Native packages are unsigned. Code signing, notarization, and Apple Silicon
  packaging remain future release work.
- MCP mutations require explicit server opt-in and retain proposal, review,
  revision, apply, and durable-receipt protections.
